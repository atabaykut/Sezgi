What is Sezgi?

It reads your application's code: it understands what the app is and what each operation affects, explains this to AI with evidence, and lets the AI work on it safely.

Every fact carries a trust stamp: certain, observed, inferred or unknown. In live mode every AI request passes a policy check: the token's permission, the map's freshness, an input preview, the operation's trust stamp and its effects. A delete, an effect on an external system, a write that cannot be undone, evidence that is not certain or a rule the preview cannot check needs human approval. Sezgi says plainly what it does not know. Everything runs on this computer.

Write in English or Turkish. Sezgi maps the request to this app's operations; each result says what it affects, whether it can be undone and whether approval is needed. Nothing is run.

Which operations will you open to the AI?

      
All are closed by default. A token is created from the ones you check; an AI connected with that token sees only the operations you chose (search, describe, preview, call). The level comes from the kind of operation: read (GET), write (POST/PUT/PATCH), destructive (DELETE, human approval every time).
